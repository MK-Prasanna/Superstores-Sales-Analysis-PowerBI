# Superstore Sales Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive, executive-facing Power BI dashboard engineered to analyze historical retail data, track core financial health metrics, and isolate seasonal sales trend distributions.

## 💡 Key Business Insights Generated
* **High-Level Financials:** Successfully tracked over ₹2.3M in total revenue and ₹286K in total net profit.
* **Profitability Health Check:** Calculated an overall baseline business profit margin of 12.47%.
* **Category Performance:** Isolated and visualized high-performing sales items versus low-margin operations across corporate product categories.
* **Temporal Trend Mapping:** Mapped monthly sales cycles to reveal clear peak-and-valley performance quarters.
